Large-context hatch U-Net, research_v5/largecontext
HGB baseline on the 55 development queries: best document-macro IoU 0.657, and precision 0.198 at recall >= 0.98.

Model
Half-resolution input (2x2 area mean), 1024x1024 crops, 4 channels (RGB plus ink = 1 - luminance).
Query crop is halved the same way and repeated, not stretched, into 96x96.
U-Net, 5 stride-2 stages, channels 32-64-128-192-256, three dilation-2 layers at the bottom.
Query conditioning at 1/8 and 1/16: FiLM from a 128-d vector, plus max cosine similarity against the 12x12 query map.
Output is one logit per 2x2 half-resolution block, which is one cell per 4x4 native pixels.
Parameters 5,520,545. Float32. AdamW lr 3e-4, weight decay 1e-4, grad clip 1.0, cosine decay, seed 1234, batch 2.
Loss is masked BCE + soft Dice on cells whose native pixels are at least 75% reviewed-known. Target is the positive area fraction.
Augmentation: identical 90-degree turns and flips on the drawing crop and the query crop.
Sampling: fit split only for run 1 (artifacts/splits/fit.json, real and generated_cad), document-balanced, half the crops centered on a positive reviewed pixel and half on a known negative.
