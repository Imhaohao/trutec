Auto-context stage 3 on the frozen v6 stacker.
Stage 3 reads cross-fitted stage-2 score maps in square windows so a cell can be raised or lowered by what stage 2 thinks of its neighbourhood.
Hyperparameters were chosen by grouped cross-validation on fit folds only.
