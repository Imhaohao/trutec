Hatch region experiment findings
Development split only: 55 real queries, 12 documents. Scored by research_v5/devharness.py.
Targets: document-macro IoU >= 0.95, pooled recall >= 0.98, pooled precision >= 0.95, reviewed-negative false-positive rate <= 0.05.
No variant met all targets.

LABEL RULE
Oracle votes use development labels and are not a deployable method. A region is positive when strictly more than half of its reviewed pixels are positive. Ties stay negative. The partition itself never reads labels, document ids, or reviewed coordinates.
Deployable scores read the drawing, the query box, and the fit-split texture model. Region statistics are computed on the full stride-4 grid, then copied onto the pixels the harness asks about.

SHARED SETTINGS
Ink pixel: darkest RGB channel <= 255-32 (deficit_threshold=32). Structuring element: ellipse. Paper components: 4-connected. Filled blobs: 8-connected. Superpixel blur radius: 17. Watershed seeds sit on a cell grid and snap to the lowest texture-gradient pixel in a local window. Texture model: models/models/texture-hgb-crop-v1.joblib, crop descriptor, stride 4, 46 phase-invariant channels. Full-grid probabilities cached as float16 in research_v5/region/cache/probs/. Official pixel baseline re-scored from artifacts/texture-crop-development-v1/probabilities.
Cosine and correlation scores are mapped with (similarity+1)/2, so 1 matches the query, 0.5 is orthogonal, and 0 is the opposite pattern. Empty regions and flat regions score 0. Mass rank is the fraction of stride-grid cells that sit in a lower-scoring region of the same drawing.
Mixes: min, average, and geometric mean of the pixel score and the region score. Pattern-match pass also tried max and product. Choosing among these global formulas is development-selected.

Reading of the oracle: a 48-pixel watershed superpixel is fine enough. If every superpixel could be labeled by a majority vote, document-macro IoU would be 0.9975 with recall 0.9992 and precision 0.9994. A 96-pixel cell is almost the same (0.9950). A 192-pixel cell drops to 0.9091. The best wall enclosure (open 1, close 8) reaches 0.7968 IoU with recall 0.8281, which misses the 0.98 recall target even with perfect votes. Closing by 4 and treating each blob as a region reaches precision 0.9421 at recall 0.7587. Paper left after deleting thin strokes is precise (0.9542) and misses most of the hatch (recall 0.4795), because the strokes themselves fall into neighboring paper that is mostly negative.

WHAT FAILED
Averaging, taking quantiles, or taking the fraction of cells above 0.25 or 0.50 inside a superpixel makes the score a bit cleaner than the raw pixel score, and it still fires on text, linework, and other hatches. At recall >= 0.98 the best of these is precision 0.258538.
Running the same HGB on a region's median 46-d descriptor (median_feature_hgb) is worse than averaging the pixel probabilities. On 48-pixel cells its best macro IoU is 0.634086. On the open1-close8 enclosure the best point is the base rate: macro IoU 0.400867, recall 1.000000, precision 0.145217. A large enclosure mixes the query hatch with other ink, so one descriptor cannot vote for the whole enclosure.
Gabor relative match on 48-pixel cells is the most precise region-only score and it does not cover the hatch. best: thr=0.83 macro_iou=0.472927 recall=0.552456 precision=0.597298 fpr=0.063278 tp=277996 fp=187427 fn=225204 tn=2774537. held: thr=0.04 macro_iou=0.414776 recall=0.986461 precision=0.160917 fpr=0.873866 tp=496387 fp=2588359 fn=6813 tn=373605.
Full-vector cosine, centered correlation, 6-bin orientation correlation, and 3-bin wavelength correlation, including within-drawing mass ranks and max/product mixes with the pixel score, did not beat the probability average. The best of that pass is product_pixel__cosine_full on 48-pixel cells: macro IoU 0.659513, and precision 0.200774 at recall >= 0.98. Orientation correlation alone stays at the base rate (best macro IoU 0.426369, precision 0.152537, recall 0.937079), so the query's dominant line angle is shared by a lot of other ink on the same sheet.
Min, average, geometric mean, max, and product of pixel and region scores move the operating point along the same curve. They do not create the missing separation between the query hatch and other ink.
Wall enclosures and filled blobs are the wrong grain for this model. Their oracle ceiling is already below the recall target, and painting one score on a mixed enclosure throws away the pixel model's best operating point (enclosure avg_pixel_region__prob_mean best macro IoU 0.651255, below the pixel cache's 0.656818).

CONCLUSION
Region geometry can be fine enough: 48-pixel texture superpixels are almost pure, and a perfect vote on them would clear every target (macro IoU 0.997475, recall 0.999235, precision 0.999445). The texture model cannot tell which of those regions match the query once recall has to stay at 0.98. The best measured deployable gain is 0.010825 macro IoU and 0.060215 precision at that recall, and that is the ceiling of this model plus these partitions.
