Conclusion
The ratio heads are not passed through the sigmoid. That sentence in the shared settings applies to the logistic and SVM heads only.

Best variant: knn_k1_trim_max9.
Drop the farthest 30% of query cells from their median, whiten by the unlabeled mean and std, score each grid cell by (distance to 96 unlabeled prototypes) / (distance to the nearest of 96 query prototypes + that unlabeled distance), then take a 9x9 maximum (72 px) on the stride-8 score grid and bilinear-sample it.
best macro IoU 0.791120 at threshold 0.59 (precision 0.511786, recall 0.887691, reviewed-negative FPR 0.143861).
best precision at recall >= 0.98 is 0.220700 at threshold 0.50 (macro IoU 0.606520, recall 0.980596, FPR 0.588238).
all_goals_met: False.
The highest macro IoU of any variant is knn_k5_max9 at 0.793201, but its precision at recall >= 0.98 is 0.193396, which is below the HGB cache (0.198323).

What worked: k-NN against the query cells, trimming overlay cells, and a 72 px max so the paper between strokes inherits a nearby score. A 7200x4800 sheet took about 21-25 s to describe.

What failed: logistic regression and linear SVM fall to the base rate (precision 0.145217) once recall must reach 0.98, and widening the sigmoid did not fix that. Grayscale closing scored worse than the plain max (best close IoU 0.739). Fusing the dilated k-NN with the HGB cache lowered both the best IoU and the high-recall precision, because HGB is low on the paper between strokes. Neighborhoods of 56 px and 88 px are close; 120 px over-grows.
