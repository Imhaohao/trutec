1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Evaluating label-free color features on the development split (55 queries across 12 CAD documents) demonstrates that local color gating provides significant and broad gains for grayscale matchers (HGB, CNN, and AND fusion).
Color evidence successfully eliminates cross-color false alarms (e.g. red hatches firing on yellow linework, or black hatches firing on colored annotations) without harming achromatic queries or pure black-and-white sheets.

6. OBSERVATIONS AND TAKEAWAYS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Gating is 100% safe on achromatic drawings: 10 out of 12 documents are purely achromatic or
   have zero color mismatch, resulting in exactly zero false suppression (neutral impact).
2. High-impact elimination of multi-color confusion: In multi-color documents (doc-20843a7f8250490b
   and doc-6da06a6d644b074f), color gating removes 39,699 false positives, boosting document macro
   IoU by up to +0.48 on colored queries while also protecting achromatic queries on the same sheets.
3. Gating strength robust: Strengths alpha in [0.6, 0.8] provide consistent peak performance.
