Hatch Matching Challenge: technical report for the submitted system (v14)

Summary

v14 scores 0.9077 document-macro IoU, 0.9742 pooled recall and 0.7419 pooled precision on all 57 public-validation queries across 13 documents, measured by the unchanged official evaluator. The reviewed-negative false-positive rate is 0.0561. These numbers are validation-informed (see "Validation exposure"). Private-test and whole-sheet accuracy are unknown.

Input/output contract

infer_v14.py reads a label-free request manifest (id, image, width, height, query_box, context_boxes) and writes one binary PNG per request at the original drawing size into --output-dir; 255 marks the matching hatch. Annotation fields are stripped on load: masks, rectangles, expected counts, document ids and private labels are never read during inference. A runtime.json with per-drawing timing is written next to the masks.

Method

1. Query cleaning (rule g2): text glyphs and isolated lines are removed from the query crop; families of parallel or connected strokes are kept as the pattern.
2. Stage 1: a histogram gradient-boosting classifier (HGB, 200 rounds, 31 leaves) compares a 184-feature texture descriptor of each location with the cleaned query descriptor.
3. Stage 2: a larger HGB (600 rounds, up to 63 leaves) decides each 8-pixel cell from 144 features: stage-1 score statistics in windows of 16-256 px around the cell (so text and dimension lines printed over a hatch stay with it), ink and colour fractions, and orientation, line-spacing and mark-size comparisons with the query. Hard negatives (reviewed negatives that stage 1 scored >= 0.75) carry three times the weight in training.
4. A fixed ink-colour gate lowers scores where the local ink colour disagrees with the query. Cells scoring >= 0.25 are written as positive at native resolution.

Training data and selection

Dataset release: dataset-v1. Evaluator revision: 65b98e480f2a10b82f974f4feb519ee4e012d66c. Both stages were trained on the public training split only: the fit partition (357 queries, 105 documents: 50 real, 55 organizer-generated CAD) plus the 55 real development queries (12 documents). No external data, pretrained weights or synthetic data of our own are used by v14. Stage 1 was trained with 4 document-grouped cross-fit folds so that stage-2 training cells carry out-of-fold stage-1 scores.

Selection: the query cleaner was chosen on cross-fitted scores of real training drawings; the hard-negative weighting by document-grouped cross-validation; the 0.25 threshold and colour gate on the development split (v7). The stage-2 size was chosen by document-grouped 4-fold cross-validation over the training documents with a rule fixed before running (a larger model replaces the recipe only if cross-validated macro IoU rises by at least 0.01): recipe 0.755, 600 rounds/63 leaves 0.780, 800 rounds/127 leaves 0.779. On the 62 real training documents alone, the chosen model raised out-of-fold macro IoU from 0.789 to 0.818 and precision from 0.544 to 0.626. v14 was declared the primary submission before it was trained or scored (PROGRESS.md checkpoint 22).

Validation exposure

Public validation was scored for v1-v3 and v6-v14 (twelve versions), and earlier validation errors informed which directions were explored. No v14 setting was selected on public validation, and v14 was frozen and hashed (artifacts/frozen-pipeline-v14.json, 75 files) before its single validation run. Because several versions were scored on the same 13 documents, the best of them is likely somewhat optimistic; a document bootstrap of the v12 result gave a 95% range of 0.84-0.97 IoU.

Limitations and failure cases

- Look-alike patterns: the main error is whole neighbouring regions hatched with a similar pattern (same angle and spacing, different material). Development experiments with FFT angle-spacing spectra, learned embeddings (DINO, contrastive), sibling-query competition and synthetic trap sheets did not separate them reliably on real drawings.
- Recall 0.974 is below 0.98; raising the threshold trades recall for precision and lowered real-document IoU in cross-validation.
- Real training and validation annotations are sparse reviewed windows; precision outside reviewed windows is not measured.
- Only macOS arm64 has been measured.
